# Navigate Functional Overview

## Project Purpose

Navigate is a history education website designed to help students study U.S. History, World History, and European History through organized lessons, videos, quizzes, downloadable materials, live tutoring, progress tracking, and learning rewards.

The goal of the platform is to make history easier to understand, easier to review, and more accessible for students who want guided support outside the classroom.

## Main Audience

Navigate is built for:

- Students studying history courses.
- Students preparing for tests, quizzes, or exams.
- Learners who benefit from guided review and visual resources.
- Students who want live tutoring or group study support.
- Families or educators looking for structured history resources.

## Primary User Experience

1. A student visits the home page and learns what Navigate offers.
2. The student chooses a course area: U.S. History, World History, or European History.
3. The student studies with lessons, videos, quizzes, and downloadable notes.
4. The student can book live tutoring or group study sessions.
5. The student can track progress through the dashboard.
6. The student earns points, achievement levels, certificates, and rewards for learning activity.
7. The student can ask Navigate Bot for help or message tutors for support.

## Website Features

### 1. Home Page

The home page introduces Navigate as a history learning platform. It uses historical imagery, a clear navigation bar, and sections that explain the purpose of the site.

Key features:

- Welcomes users to the platform.
- Explains Navigate's mission and learning value.
- Guides users toward services, schedule, dashboard, resources, and about pages.
- Presents history learning as interactive, supportive, and student-friendly.

### 2. Site Navigation

Navigate uses a consistent top navigation experience across the website so students can move between major sections easily.

Key features:

- Provides quick access to Services, Schedule, Dashboard, Resources, and About.
- Gives users account access through Sign In and Sign Up.
- Keeps major learning areas easy to find from every page.
- Supports a consistent browsing experience across the full site.

### 3. Account Access

Students can create an account or sign in to access personalized features.

Key features:

- Allows students to register for an account.
- Allows returning students to sign in.
- Supports personalized dashboard information.
- Connects learning progress, sessions, and rewards to the student experience.
- Allows users to sign out when finished.

### 4. Guest Booking

Navigate also supports limited guest access for students who want to book quickly without creating a full account first.

Key features:

- Lets guests begin the session booking process.
- Explains the benefits of creating an account.
- Encourages account creation for full access to rewards, badges, progress, and saved learning history.

### 5. Resources Hub

The Resources section is the central learning roadmap for the website. It organizes the main academic materials by subject area.

Key features:

- Lets students choose between U.S. History, World History, and European History.
- Connects each course to lessons, videos, quizzes, and downloads.
- Presents each subject as a guided roadmap.
- Helps students find the right type of resource based on what they need: explanation, review, practice, or study notes.

### 6. U.S. History Resources

The U.S. History section focuses on early American history and major historical developments.

Featured learning topics include:

- Indigenous Americans before European contact.
- Spanish colonization of the Americas.
- The Columbian Exchange.

Student benefits:

- Builds understanding of early American societies.
- Connects exploration, colonization, exchange, and cultural change.
- Provides multiple learning formats for the same subject area.

### 7. World History Resources

The World History section focuses on ancient civilizations and global historical foundations.

Featured learning topics include:

- Ancient India.
- Ancient Egypt.
- Ancient Mesopotamia.

Student benefits:

- Helps students understand early civilizations.
- Connects geography, government, culture, religion, writing, and technology.
- Supports review through lessons, videos, quizzes, and downloadable notes.

### 8. European History Resources

The European History section focuses on major early and medieval European developments.

Featured learning topics include:

- The Fall of the Roman Empire.
- The Rise of Christianity.
- Feudalism and Manorialism.

Student benefits:

- Helps students understand transitions from ancient Rome to medieval Europe.
- Explains political, religious, social, and economic change.
- Supports review with multiple resource types.

### 9. Lessons

Lessons provide structured explanations of history topics. They are designed to help students learn key ideas before reviewing or testing themselves.

Key features:

- Presents subject-specific history lessons.
- Uses visual and presentation-style learning materials.
- Organizes lessons by course area.
- Helps students review major people, places, events, causes, and effects.
- Supports progress tracking for completed learning.

### 10. Videos

The video section gives students curated history videos organized by subject.

Key features:

- Provides video-based review for U.S. History, World History, and European History.
- Helps visual and auditory learners reinforce topics.
- Gives students another way to study lesson content.
- Includes source and citation information for educational transparency.

### 11. Quizzes

The quiz section allows students to practice and check their understanding.

Key features:

- Provides quizzes for U.S. History, World History, and European History.
- Uses multiple-choice questions.
- Gives immediate feedback.
- Shows score results.
- Helps students identify what they understand and what they should review again.
- Contributes to progress and rewards.

### 12. Downloads

The downloads section gives students study materials they can save and review outside the website.

Key features:

- Provides downloadable notes for U.S. History, World History, and European History.
- Lets students preview subject study materials.
- Supports offline studying.
- Gives students a convenient review tool for tests and assignments.

### 13. Dashboard

The dashboard gives students a personalized view of their learning progress.

Key features:

- Welcomes the signed-in student.
- Shows progress across learning activities.
- Displays completed or recent activity.
- Helps students understand what they have worked on.
- Encourages continued study through progress visibility.
- Includes achievements and rewards information.

Dashboard activity can include:

- Lessons completed.
- Videos watched.
- Quizzes taken.
- Downloads accessed.
- Live sessions attended.

### 14. Progress Tracking

Navigate tracks student learning activity so students can see growth over time.

Key features:

- Tracks completed lessons.
- Tracks watched videos.
- Tracks quiz results.
- Tracks downloaded study materials.
- Tracks live session participation.
- Shows recent activity and overall progress.
- Helps students stay aware of what they have already studied.

### 15. Rewards and Achievements

The rewards system makes learning more motivating by giving students points, levels, certificates, and redeemable rewards.

Key features:

- Awards points for learning activities.
- Encourages consistent studying.
- Recognizes progress through achievement levels.
- Provides certificates for reaching major milestones.
- Allows students to redeem points for educational rewards.

Achievement levels include:

- Stone Tablet Starter.
- Ancient Archivist.
- Medieval Mastermind.
- Age of Exploration Expert.
- Revolutionary Scholar.
- Historian Supreme.

Reward examples include:

- History timelines.
- Primary source collections.
- Documentary access.
- Virtual museum experiences.
- Study guides.
- Expert session rewards.

### 16. Live Sessions

Live Sessions allow students to schedule tutoring or group study support.

Key features:

- Shows upcoming booked sessions.
- Lets students book a new session.
- Lets students choose a session type.
- Lets students choose a history subject.
- Lets students select a date and time.
- Lets students reschedule or cancel sessions.
- Lets students join a session from the session card.

Session types include:

- Live Tutoring for one-on-one support.
- Group Study for collaborative review.

Supported subjects include:

- World History.
- European History.
- Ancient History.
- Government and Politics.
- U.S. History.

### 17. Schedule

The Schedule page helps students view upcoming history sessions and booked learning events in a calendar format.

Key features:

- Displays sessions by date.
- Gives students a calendar-based view of upcoming learning.
- Connects students to live session booking.
- Helps students plan study time around scheduled support.

### 18. Services

The Services page summarizes the support options Navigate provides.

Key features:

- Explains tutoring services.
- Highlights available learning support.
- Connects students to session booking.
- Connects students to Navigate Bot.
- Connects students to tutor messaging.

### 19. Navigate Bot

Navigate Bot is a help assistant for quick website and learning support questions.

Key features:

- Answers basic questions about scheduling.
- Directs students to resources and materials.
- Explains dashboard access.
- Helps users understand where to go next.
- Supports voice input and spoken responses when available.

### 20. Tutor Messenger

The tutor messaging feature lets students send questions or leave messages for tutor support.

Key features:

- Lets students type messages to tutors.
- Shows whether tutors are currently available.
- Gives an offline message when tutors are outside business hours.
- Supports voice input and spoken responses when available.
- Helps students ask for support even when they are not in a live session.

### 21. Profile

The profile area gives students a place to view account-related and learning-related information.

Key features:

- Shows student-facing account information.
- Connects to rewards and progress.
- Supports a personalized learning experience.
- Helps students see their achievements and activity.

### 22. About Page

The About page explains Navigate's mission and identity.

Key features:

- Describes Navigate as an educational nonprofit initiative.
- Explains the purpose behind the platform.
- Introduces the team.
- Provides context about why the site was created.

### 23. Accessibility

Navigate includes accessibility-focused content and design choices to make the site easier to use for more students.

Key features:

- Provides an accessibility statement.
- Supports keyboard-friendly navigation.
- Includes visible focus and skip navigation support.
- Uses readable layouts and clear labels.
- Supports voice features in chat tools when browser support is available.

### 24. Credits and Legal Pages

Navigate includes pages for attribution, privacy, terms, and accessibility information.

Key features:

- Credits educational videos, images, resources, and third-party materials.
- Explains privacy expectations.
- Provides terms of service.
- Documents accessibility goals and support.

## Functional Limitations

- Student data is meant for the current browser experience and is not designed as a full multi-device school information system.
- The chatbot provides guided support, not full human tutoring.
- Tutor messaging gives simulated or delayed support rather than guaranteed instant live responses.
- Live sessions depend on the available meeting link and scheduled time.
- Rewards are motivational learning tools and not official academic grades.

## Functional Summary

Navigate is a complete student-facing history learning website. It helps students discover resources, study major history topics, practice with quizzes, download notes, schedule tutoring, track learning progress, and stay motivated through rewards. Its main value is combining history content, tutoring access, and student progress tools into one organized learning experience.
