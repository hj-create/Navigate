flag Our Purpose

Navigate is a history learning hub created by students, for students. The site brings together live tutoring, group study opportunities, progress tracking, and curated resources so learners can explore history in a more engaging and accessible way. Students can book sessions on the schedule, monitor completed activities on the dashboard, and use lessons, videos, quizzes, and downloadable materials from the resources page.

stars Why Choose Us?

Navigate supports peer-to-peer learning through student-built tools, approachable history roadmaps, and collaborative live sessions. The chatbot helps answer quick questions about scheduling, resources, and next steps, while the dashboard helps students stay motivated by showing their progress over time.
